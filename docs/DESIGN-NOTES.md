# Design decisions

The site lets the house, water and weather do the persuading: a full-bleed photograph of the Old Manse opens the home page, followed by short editorial sections rather than a stack of competing widgets. The palette is taken from granite, weathered window paint and Atlantic grey—warm paper, deep green-grey and a muted blue—with Cormorant Garamond for a light, unhurried voice and DM Sans for practical information. There is one persistent booking destination, repeated only where a decision is natural; room rates, policies and travel times are plain text or simple tables, not badges. The history is told through the specific 1746 encounter and the 1880s rebuild, with missing interior details visibly left for the owner. Static Astro pages, local fonts, responsive AVIF/WebP photographs, semantic landmarks, visible focus, reduced-motion support and an honest mail-app enquiry form keep the experience quiet, fast and maintainable.
